The Artist Behind The Lens

Hi, I’m Elizabeth Schuelke.

I’m a photographer, teacher, mom, and Navasota girl who has loved taking pictures since my junior year of high school. What started as a hobby quickly became something I couldn’t imagine not doing.

I’ve always loved photographs, but what I love even more is what they give us years later. A picture that takes you right back to a person, a season, a feeling, or a moment you didn’t realize would mean so much.

I love the in-between moments. The laughs you didn’t plan, the way your kids look at you, the little details you’ll forget with time, and the people who make your life what it is.

My goal is simple: to give you beautiful photographs, genuine moments, and memories you can hold onto forever. I’ll probably tell you to forget I’m even there, let your kids be kids, laugh at each other, and just enjoy being together. The best photos usually happen that way anyway.

This is what I love to do, and I’m so glad you’re here.
